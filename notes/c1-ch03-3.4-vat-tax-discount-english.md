## 1. VAT and discount meaning

Discount reduces the customer's payable amount. VAT or tax is a charge added to the amount. We apply the discount first, then VAT.

```python
gross_amount = 10000
discount_rate = 0.10
vat_rate = 0.15

discount = gross_amount * discount_rate
subtotal = gross_amount - discount
vat = subtotal * vat_rate
total = subtotal + vat

print(discount)
print(subtotal)
print(vat)
print(total)
```

**Output**

```text
1000.0
9000.0
1350.0
10350.0
```

The discount is removed first. VAT is then applied to the reduced subtotal.

## 2. Gross amount

Gross amount is the sales value before discount and VAT. The formula is `quantity * unit_price`.

```python
quantity = 4
unit_price = 2500
gross_amount = quantity * unit_price

print(f"Gross: {gross_amount:.2f} taka")
```

**Output**

```text
Gross: 10000.00 taka
```

Four items at 2500 taka each produce 10000 taka gross.

## 3. Discount amount

Convert the discount percentage into a rate first. Ten percent is `0.10`. The formula is `discount = gross * rate`.

```python
gross_amount = 10000
discount_percentage = 10
discount_rate = discount_percentage / 100
discount = gross_amount * discount_rate

print(discount)
```

**Output**

```text
1000.0
```

Dividing 10 by 100 gives 0.10. Ten percent of 10000 is 1000.

## 4. Discounted subtotal

The amount after discount is the subtotal. The formula is `subtotal = gross - discount`.

```python
gross_amount = 10000
discount = 1000
subtotal = gross_amount - discount

print(f"Subtotal: {subtotal:.2f} taka")
```

**Output**

```text
Subtotal: 9000.00 taka
```

The gross amount is reduced before VAT is calculated.

## 5. VAT amount

Apply the VAT rate to the subtotal. The formula is `VAT = subtotal * VAT rate`.

```python
subtotal = 9000
vat_percentage = 15
vat_rate = vat_percentage / 100
vat = subtotal * vat_rate

print(f"VAT: {vat:.2f} taka")
```

**Output**

```text
VAT: 1350.00 taka
```

The discounted subtotal is 9000. Fifteen percent is 1350.

## 6. Final invoice total

The final total is subtotal plus VAT. The formula is `total = subtotal + VAT`.

```python
subtotal = 9000
vat = 1350
final_total = subtotal + vat

print(f"Final total: {final_total:.2f} taka")
```

**Output**

```text
Final total: 10350.00 taka
```

The customer pays the subtotal and the VAT together.

## 7. Converting a tax rate

A report may show 5, 10 or 15. Calculation needs the percentage divided by 100.

```python
tax_percentage = 5
tax_rate = tax_percentage / 100
tax_base = 20000
tax_amount = tax_base * tax_rate

print(tax_rate)
print(tax_amount)
```

**Output**

```text
0.05
1000.0
```

Five percent is 0.05. Five percent of 20000 is 1000.

## 8. A zero-value guard

Zero gross amount produces no useful invoice. A negative amount is suspicious, so use a comparison before continuing.

```python
gross_amount = 0

if gross_amount <= 0:
    print("Check the gross amount")
else:
    print("Invoice calculation can continue")
```

**Output**

```text
Check the gross amount
```

The gross amount is not positive, so calculation does not continue.

## 9. Calculation order

Changing the order changes the result. In this lesson, VAT is calculated on the discounted subtotal, not on gross.

```python
gross_amount = 10000
discount = 1000
vat_rate = 0.15

subtotal = gross_amount - discount
vat = subtotal * vat_rate
final_total = subtotal + vat

print(f"Subtotal: {subtotal:.2f}")
print(f"VAT: {vat:.2f}")
print(f"Total: {final_total:.2f}")
```

**Output**

```text
Subtotal: 9000.00
VAT: 1350.00
Total: 10350.00
```

The lines show the discount effect, VAT, and final payable total.

## 10. An invoice summary

Now combine quantity, price, discount and VAT in one report.

```python
quantity = 4
unit_price = 2500
discount_percentage = 10
vat_percentage = 15

gross = quantity * unit_price
discount = gross * discount_percentage / 100
subtotal = gross - discount
vat = subtotal * vat_percentage / 100
total = subtotal + vat

print(f"Gross: {gross:.2f}")
print(f"Discount: {discount:.2f}")
print(f"Subtotal: {subtotal:.2f}")
print(f"VAT: {vat:.2f}")
print(f"Total: {total:.2f}")
```

**Output**

```text
Gross: 10000.00
Discount: 1000.00
Subtotal: 9000.00
VAT: 1350.00
Total: 10350.00
```

Each line shows one invoice stage. Staged calculations are easier to audit.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem 1: VAT is too high

- What is happening: VAT exceeds the expected amount.
- Most likely cause: VAT was applied to gross before discount.
- Fix: calculate subtotal first, then use `subtotal * vat_rate`.
- Prevention: keep invoice stages in separate variables.

### Problem 2: The discount is ten times too large

- What is happening: a 10% discount looks equal to, or larger than, gross.
- Most likely cause: `10` was used as the rate.
- Fix: set `discount_rate = discount_percentage / 100`.
- Prevention: use separate names for percentage and rate.

### Problem 3: Discount increased the final total

- What is happening: discount increases the payable amount.
- Most likely cause: `subtotal = gross + discount` was written.
- Fix: use `subtotal = gross - discount`.
- Prevention: remember that discount reduces, while VAT adds.

### Problem 4: The invoice total is negative

- What is happening: the final amount falls below zero.
- Most likely cause: discount exceeds 100%, or input is negative.
- Fix: validate the percentage and gross amount.
- Prevention: define valid business ranges first.

## Common Misconceptions

1. **Wrong:** VAT always uses gross → **Truth:** the policy defines the base; this lesson uses subtotal.
2. **Wrong:** 15 is the calculation rate → **Truth:** 15% becomes 0.15.
3. **Wrong:** discount is added to the total → **Truth:** discount reduces the payable amount.
4. **Wrong:** subtotal and final total are the same → **Truth:** final total includes VAT.
5. **Wrong:** calculation order never matters → **Truth:** changing the base changes the result.

## Quiz

### 1. What does a discount normally do?

a. It reduces the payable amount  
b. It increases the VAT rate

**Answer: a.** A discount reduces what the customer pays.

### 2. Which is a 15% rate?

**Answer: b.** Divide the percentage by 100.

### 3. What is the amount after discount?

b. Gross divided by discount  
c. Gross minus discount  

**Answer: c.** Subtracting discount gives subtotal.

### 4. What is the VAT base in this lesson?

**Answer: a.** This lesson applies discount first.

a. `9000`

**Answer: a.** `10000 - 1000 = 9000`.

a. `7650`

**Answer: b.** Add VAT to subtotal.

**Answer: a.** A different base gives a different VAT amount.

## Key Idea

Build an invoice in stages: gross, discount, subtotal, VAT, final total. Discount reduces. VAT adds. Convert percentages into rates before calculating.

**Rhyme 1:** Reduce with discount, and keep subtotal right.  
**Rhyme 2:** Add VAT afterwards, and the invoice comes out right.
